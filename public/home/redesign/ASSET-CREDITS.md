Self-hosted DM Sans and Libre Caslon Display fonts come from Google Fonts. Their SIL Open Font License notices are included in `fonts/`. Font subsets retain Latin characters, accents and common punctuation. System fonts remain available as fallbacks.

Public pages share `resources/views/layouts/investment-home.blade.php`. Page-specific views and reusable page sections live under `resources/views/home/`. Existing routes, controllers, authentication, service records, plan terms, contact details, certificates, translation and live chat settings are reused. Stylesheets and scripts load directly from `public/home/redesign/`; no new npm dependencies or build step are required.
